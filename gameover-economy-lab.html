The constraint

Infinite time can renew participation. It cannot make one ETH both permanently untouched and spendable. This lab exposes that trade-off instead of hiding it.

Add external revenue

Hard limit: an ETH-only reserve cannot guarantee its original USDC value if ETH itself falls below the deposit price. The guardrail can stop sales; it cannot stop market loss. A real guarantee needs stable collateral, insurance or another external source of value.

Why the score resets

Daily +1/−1 positions are temporary coordination signals, not a hoardable token.

  • Ending net surplus determines eligibility.
  • Square-root weighting gives diminishing rewards for many plusses.
  • Counterparty diversity makes repeated pair activity worth less than wider cooperation.
  • Claims and reputation persist after balances return to zero.
  • Perpetual Commons Debt never falls when somebody claims.

Rules made visible

Economic model

This second MVP separates the renewable encounter game, the protected reserve and externally funded claims.

Encounter layer

  • Actors start each UTC epoch at zero.
  • Contributor +1; recipient −1.
  • A recipient at −1 must contribute before receiving again.
  • AI encounters require an independent human witness.
  • Raw evidence stays off-chain; hashes remain.

Distribution layer

  • Only positive humans are eligible.
  • Net, not gross, activity is counted.
  • Weight = √net × √(unique counterparties ÷ encounters).
  • Revenue always distributes when eligible users exist.
  • Appreciation distributes only above the high-water mark.

Commons layer

  • Initial reserve is a simulated 1 ETH.
  • Future live experiment begins with 0.01 ETH.
  • Donors gain recognition, never a withdrawal claim.
  • The protected USDC book floor and debt never decrease.
  • Each claimant initiates their own cash-out.

High-water algorithm

Only value above the preceding gross peak counts as new appreciation. The selected log-dampened version starts near a 25% share for small gains and declines as a percentage when gains become large.

newGain = max(0, reserveValue − highWater)logHarvest = min(buffer, 0.25 × floor × ln(1 + newGain / floor))postHarvestReserve ≥ protectedFloor

What the algorithm cannot do

Selling appreciated ETH necessarily reduces the number of ETH in the pool. The model can preserve a USDC floor or preserve the full ETH quantity; it cannot fund payouts from price appreciation while guaranteeing both. Staking and agent revenue avoid that specific conflict because they are external inflows.
