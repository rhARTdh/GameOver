The flipped time-value premise

Separate the thing that expires from the thing that endures.

Capacity is most useful now.

Today’s +1/−1 position returns to zero at sunrise, so hoarding does not compound.

The future underwrites one present step.

A verified person may move to −1 once; further receiving requires contribution first.

History—not the token—accumulates.

The non-speculative asset is a durable trail of confirmed participation.

Value is not conjured.

The protocol can issue permission endlessly; it cannot create fruit, labour or trust.

Translate the metaphor into buildable components

The strongest hackathon version is precise about what each phrase can and cannot mean.

Your phrase Runnable interpretation Why this boundary matters
Infinite central depository A formula that renews bounded capacity each epoch; no treasury and no pre-minted supply. Ethereum integers are finite, and unlimited redeemable money would destroy scarcity.
AI sees the exchange AI structures submitted evidence and assigns a digest; two humans remain the primary witnesses. A model cannot independently know that a physical exchange happened without trusted inputs.
Time and space on-chain Block timestamp plus a salted location commitment or coarse cell. Publishing exact GPS permanently would create an avoidable surveillance system.
Encounter token A non-ERC-20 daily accounting position moved only by a mutually signed encounter. No open transfer or market means there is little reason to buy, hoard or speculate on it.
Sybil resistance

Rotating QR and biometrics can unlock a wallet, but do not alone prove one-human-one-account. Mock an allowlist now; integrate personhood later.

Collusion and empty encounters

Two signatures prove agreement, not economic substance. AI risk signals and reputation can help, but cannot eliminate coordinated fiction.

External redemption

If merchants must redeem credits for rand, someone still needs a funded reserve. Keep the first MVP about access, circulation and history.
