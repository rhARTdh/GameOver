What this demo proves and does not prove

A provider’s transaction proposes an exchange. Only the named beneficiary can confirm it during the same UTC day. Confirmation moves the daily positions and permanently records the two addresses, digest and time.

Daily positions automatically read as zero after UTC midnight; confirmed receipts and lifetime totals remain. There is no token, payment, treasury, payout, biometric check, personhood proof or AI witness in this build.

Two wallet signatures show control of two addresses and agreement with one digest. They do not independently prove that two people met or that the statement is objectively true.
