Sybil disclosure

v4 proves control of wallets and agreement with a commitment. It does not prove unique humans, objective truth or the real-world value of a contribution.

The premise

One hand washes the other.

GameOver records confirmed contribution without turning receipt into debt. A receiver stays at zero; a contributor earns today’s +1. The permanent history remains after the daily opportunity resets.

01

The contribution is directional

A→B and B→A are separate encounters. Payment itself does not earn credit. Both wallets confirm the same commitment.

02

The day is a boundary

At 00:00 UTC, unconfirmed invitations expire and daily points reset. Historical confirmations and lifetime totals remain.

03

Rewards diminish, dignity does not

Eligible payout weight grows with the square root of a contributor’s unique confirmed counterparts. Receipt carries no negative score.

04

The Commons is visibly accounted

Permanent principal, reserved budgets, unclaimed allocations and available test ETH remain separate on-chain.
